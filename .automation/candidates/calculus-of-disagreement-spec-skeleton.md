## 2. Primitives to define (frozen on §4's neutral cases BEFORE evaluating any house framework)

1. **Representation** — of an observable behaviour (candidate seed: the
   state-generated realization objects of the operational-theorem note,
   `.automation/foundations-qm/step1-category-measurability-note.md` §2).
2. **Observable commitment** — what a representation is answerable to
   (candidate seed: the separating state/effect pairing; "behaviour").
3. **Translation** — evidence-preserving map between representations
   (candidate seeds: Ledger C's admission schema; partial bijections).
4. **Weaker-than** — one representation carrying strictly less
   non-observable structure (candidate seeds: quotient/terminal-object
   ordering; MDL; flag the invariance-theorem relativity: no absolute
   weakest).
5. **Recoverable contact** — what must survive a rewrite (candidate seed:
   the minimal quotient's universal property — invariants of observable
   behaviour).
6. **Legal rewrite** — a representation change preserving recoverable
   contact (candidate seed: composition-invariance discipline; RSC-style
   no-hidden-bracketing as the confluence condition).

## 4. Neutral test cases (freeze definitions on these first — the against-the-house guard)

- Known-equivalent pair: DFA ↔ regular expressions (or: shifted-ℤ₃ table ↔
  ℤ₃ with distinguished generator — but this is house-adjacent; prefer the
  external pair for freezing, use the house pair as validation).
- Known-inequivalent pair: DFA vs PDA on a non-regular language.
- Known-partial pair: groups vs their abelianizations (lossy but lawful).
- A disagreement-that-isn't: two presentations of the same group.
- An agreement-that-isn't: the Round-5 pair (same ℤ₂ type, different
  invariant) as the sharpest in-house test.
