## The orientation koan ("nothing within / everything outwards")
Verdict (both): **faithful and safe as a *notebook compass* — but only after the math, never before.** It is a
mnemonic for *observable collapse vs generative richness* (kernel/image; trivial invariant/nontrivial dynamics;
vanishing homology over complicated complexes; bisimulation quotients; minimal realizations) — not mystical.
Two disciplines: **(a)** never use it as motivation in a write-up (introduce it *after* the mathematics that
generated it); **(b)** the "**cannot be identified in time**" clause conflates ≥4 distinct notions (coinductive
definition / observational indistinguishability / temporal trace semantics / no-instantaneous-identification) —
do not weld them into one slogan. As orientation it *does* succeed: it points at the distinguishability-form seam.
