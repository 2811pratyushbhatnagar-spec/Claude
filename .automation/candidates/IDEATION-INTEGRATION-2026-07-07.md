## 1. Number-ontology: the ε-fork   [math register: EXACT; framing: STRUCTURAL]

**Intuition (conceivable):** two chains — from above `1, 1/2, 1/3, …`, from below `ε, 2ε, 3ε, …` — reaching
toward each other; ε an "ideal minimal unit."

**Result — the whole thing decides itself on one axiom, "is ε divisible?":**
- **Branch A (ε divisible).** An **ordered field with infinitesimals**: hyperreals *ℝ, surreals **No**,
  Levi-Civita, Laurent/Hahn/Puiseux series. Realizes the *reciprocal-faces* intuition (ω ↔ 1/ω rigorous;
  from ω's scale the integers are negligible) — but has **no smallest positive element and no smallest
  infinitesimal** (ε/2 < ε always). Gives reciprocity, denies the atom.
- **Branch B (ε indivisible atom, everything a whole multiple).** Order-isomorphic to **(ℤ, +, <)**, ε ↔ 1.
  Gives the atom, but **contains no proper fractions** — "½ = half an atom" is forbidden by the axiom.
- **The conjunction (atom AND dense fractions generated from it): INCONSISTENT** — discreteness and density
  are mutually exclusive in the same region; if ε/2 exists strictly between 0 and ε then ε was neither
  indivisible nor least. A **closed-negative result, kept as data.**

**The genuinely-open relocation:** the "infinitely refinable, prime-indexed, unfolding in every direction"
intuition is **not** an infinitesimal below ℝ; it is **prime-indexed refinement**, hosted rigorously in the
**p-adics ℚ_p / ℤ_p**, the **profinite integers Ẑ = ∏_p ℤ_p**, the **adele ring 𝔸_ℚ** ("all primes at once" =
"every direction"), and **Steinitz/supernatural numbers**. This is the live direction worth building.
