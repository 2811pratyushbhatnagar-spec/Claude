## The target
The distinguishability program's endpoint (E4): the state space is a **non-simplex convex body** with the structure of
density operators on a **complex** Hilbert space. Reconstruction asks: which minimal axioms, added to the
GPT/distinguishability starting point, force exactly this — and no less (classical) or other (real/quaternionic)?

## The known axiom systems (verified against primary sources)
- **Hardy (2001), "Quantum Theory from Five Reasonable Axioms"** (arXiv:quant-ph/0101012). Finite-dimensional composite
  systems; introduces **local tomography**. Four axioms are consistent with both classical and quantum theory; the
  distinguishing one is **continuity** — *there exists a continuous reversible transformation between any two pure
  states* — which rules out **classical** probability theory. Local tomography (the composite dimension law `K=N²`)
  selects **complex** Hilbert space over real or quaternionic.
- **Chiribella–D'Ariano–Perinotti (2011), "Informational derivation of quantum theory"** (Phys. Rev. A 84, 012311;
  arXiv:1011.6451). Five informational axioms — **causality, perfect distinguishability, ideal compression, local
  distinguishability (local tomography), pure conditioning** — define a broad class of information-processing theories;
  a single further postulate, **purification** (every mixed state has an essentially unique pure dilation), singles out
  quantum theory within that class, *without* assuming Hilbert space up front.

## Where the computed results sit (the corridor, mapped)
- **E3 → E4 (classical simplex → non-simplex body):** this step is the **continuity / reversibility** axiom (Hardy) — a
  continuous, transitive, reversible action on pure states is impossible on a simplex (classical), and forces the round
  body. *(Computed side: E3 = simplex; E4 = the non-simplex target.)*
- **E7 (rebit / local tomography):** **local tomography** = the **ℝ-vs-ℂ** selector (Hardy; CDP's local
  distinguishability). E7's Wootters count (real `9<10`, complex `16=16`) **is** the local-tomography obstruction, and
  `ρ± = (I⊗I ± σy⊗σy)/4` is its canonical witness. Confirmed standard.

- **Purification (CDP):** the remaining structural axiom, **not yet touched** by the toy program — the natural next
  literature target.
