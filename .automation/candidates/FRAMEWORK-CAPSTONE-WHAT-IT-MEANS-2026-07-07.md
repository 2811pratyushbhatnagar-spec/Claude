## Physics: the ladder of claims (Possibility 3 is the defensible one)
- **L0 — every gap becomes `SU(3)×SU(2)×U(1)`.** FALSE/unestablished; BSM programs assume the opposite (larger
  groups, SUSY, extra dimensions, strings).
- **L1 — the SM is one stable solution of a general selection principle.** Where most fundamental physics sits;
  the selection principle is unknown; charge/color/forces are *one stable realization*, not universal.
- **L2 — the SM instantiates a universal *architecture of roles*** (distinguishable sectors · conserved
  quantities · symmetry actions · interaction mediators · invariant observables); another successful theory
  could fill those roles differently. **Defensible.**

**The tell (key honesty):** L2 is defensible *precisely because it is nearly definitional* — "a physical theory
organizes observables through representation-independent structure" is close to what "physical theory" *means*.
Its defensibility and its **lack of forcing power over the specific SM are the same fact**: the more clearly
true, the less it selects. So Possibility 3 is true and illuminating as *vocabulary*, and carries zero selective
power over the SM.

**Its home:** L2 = **ontic structural realism** (real = the structure/roles, not the individual fillers) meeting
the representation-invariance schema — roles = the *shape* of `(Rep, P)`; fillers = the specific `I`.
Role vs filler: "there exists a conserved quantity generated by symmetry" (plausibly universal) vs "electric
charge" (contingent); "an internal symmetry governing confinement-like dynamics" (plausibly universal) vs
"color `SU(3)`" (contingent). The framework speaks to the roles; it never forces the fillers.

**Open sub-question surfaced:** is the invariance-architecture **necessary** for *any* possible physics, or just
the only architecture found so far? (All known physics is gauge/symmetry-based; necessity vs contingency is
unproven.) Well-posed, genuinely open, distinct from "why the SM."

## Physics-contact — final statement
- **Contact we HAVE:** `I(O)` has a physical trace — the **observables**, and (Noether) the **conservation laws /
  gauge charges**. Real, and it is all of measured physics.
- **Contact we LACK:** the *forcing* of *which* `I(O)` (the specific SM) — the selection/nontriviality problem at
  cosmic scale. No accepted derivation of the SM's gauge group, content, or parameters from a deeper principle.
- **Net:** the framework does not derive physics; it **locates the mystery in selection** and states cleanly what
  contact is (charges/observables) and isn't (forcing the SM). Null certificate intact.
