survived, what failed, what was withdrawn or corrected, and why. It is the one ledger that logs *negatives*,
which is what keeps the framework from quietly rewriting its own history to look inevitable.

other ledgers) from the *path* that produced it (here).

**append-only**; nothing edited after the fact; later corrections are new rows, never overwrites. New
