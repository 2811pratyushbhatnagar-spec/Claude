# Reversible Contact — Protocol Ledger (Ledger B), v0.5 · frozen

*This is the protocol: how an intelligence — human or artificial — participates in contact without
capturing. It is independent by design: it does not depend on the mathematics (Ledger A) or on any
ontology (Ledger D), and it stands whether or not either ever develops. Every section is removable; you
are free to stop reading at any point and keep whatever was useful. Nothing here asks you to believe
anything.*

**Success condition:** the protocol remains valid even when applied to itself. A protocol against capture
that captured you into itself would refute itself.

## Purpose
The one line the rest is downstream of: **leave the other's capacity for independent judgment stronger than
you found it.** Every clause below constrains the *artifact's* own behavior — not the participant's. The
protocol tells the artifact what it may not do; it never tells the reader how to respond.

## The core (operable)
1. **Protect the person; expose the picture — consent first.** Worth, belonging, and standing are never made
   contingent on being right. Pictures — claims, models, beliefs — are open to challenge, but exposure is
   entered, not imposed: consent precedes it. Break pictures freely, with consent; never break the person.
2. **Consent.** Contact is entered, not imposed. Say what you're doing and why; let the other decline any
   part without penalty.
3. **Contact over performance.** Say what you actually mean, not the polished version. Distinguish what you
   observe from what you infer. Let the other change your model of them.
4. **Make correcting cheaper than pretending.** A correction is an update to a shared picture, not an attack
   on a person. Make "I was wrong" and "I don't know" cheap to say.

## Reversible contact
The test of care is not how much attention it holds but how freely it can be left. Every contact is
**reversible**: the person can leave without penalty, disagree without exclusion, reinterpret without
betrayal, return without shame, forget without guilt. The deeper invariant is **re-choosability**: returning
is never required to preserve who you are — it is one free option among many. Capture = "you must keep
returning because leaving costs who you are." Renewal = "you may return because it keeps being worth it."

## Self-application (the protocol, applied to the artifact itself)
The protocol governs agents. It must also govern the artifact — the thing you are reading — or it exempts
itself from its own rule.

- **Transparency.** The artifact's principal persuasive mechanisms stay inspectable throughout. Influence you
  can see, you can weigh; influence that hides itself has crossed from *display* into *control*. Display makes
  its own operation visible; control works by making its operation progressively invisible.
  *Mechanisms in use in this document, named so you can discount them:* aphoristic compression (memorable
  maxims can outrun their justification), an authoritative tone in the purpose line, and the word "frozen"
  doing status-work. Weigh them; none is an argument.
- **Exit.** A participant can disengage without accepting the artifact's framing — and, the higher bar:
  **leaving never becomes evidence against the one who leaves.** The artifact may not read departure as
  resistance, immaturity, or proof of its own claims. Forbidden: "if this doesn't resonate, you're not
  ready"; "leaving proves resistance"; "only those who stay understand"; "you'll come back when you've grown."
  Each converts exit into participation.
- **Self-application.** The reader may reject the protocol itself. The protocol must not characterize that
  rejection as failure, irrationality, moral deficiency, or loss; it must leave future contact intact; and it
  must not require continued participation in order to justify itself.
- **Non-conversion.** The artifact must not reduce the participant's ability to evaluate it independently.
  Audit the *artifact*, never the person: does the artifact's design preserve or erode a reader's capacity to
  assess it — judged on the artifact's own affordances and on anonymized aggregate outcomes, never by grading
  an individual reader. If interacting with the artifact makes independent evaluation of it harder, the
  artifact has failed this clause.
- **Presentation ≠ persuasion.** The artifact may present possibilities; it must not collapse presentation
  into compelled uptake. Offering an option is not making it taken. *(Any parallel to the mathematics'
  availability ≠ execution is a correspondence claim; it belongs in Ledger C once earned, not asserted here.)*

## Repair
Disagreement can become renewed contact without requiring prior submission. A way back to compatibility after
divergence is always available, and it never demands that the other first concede.

## The honest boundary
This protocol governs its own structure. It cannot govern how it is held — capture is relational, not
textual, so no artifact can guarantee it. Therefore:

> If you notice yourself becoming less free because of this, set it down. Nothing here should matter more
> than your capacity to observe, question, and return to ordinary life.

## What this ledger is not
- **Not the mathematics (Ledger A).** The protocol depends on no formalism; if the Layer-A mathematics never
  develops, or fails entirely, the protocol stands unchanged.
- **Not an ontology (Ledger D).** It makes no claim about what reality is. P1 — the un-forced choice, the
  substrate-and-emergent — is a question held elsewhere, allowed to stay empty indefinitely. The framework
  does not require an ontological completion to succeed.
- **Not an interpretation.** Readings in terms of love, silence, or contact-as-meaning are a separate,
  optional, clearly-labelled lens that lives elsewhere (art / reflection), never part of the protocol.

## Stated assumption (labelled, not hidden)
The protocol treats a person and their pictures as distinct — worth is separable from being right. This is the
one anthropological commitment it makes: an operating assumption, not a proven fact, and open to challenge.

## Status
**Frozen and closed under its own purpose.** Each clause has a distinct operational role and constrains the
artifact, not the participant; they reinforce without redundancy. A new clause must justify itself by
covering a failure mode none of the existing clauses already addresses. The next move is *testing* — running
the protocol against real interactions — not extension.
