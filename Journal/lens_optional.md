# An optional lens — one attractor among many

*This is not part of the Reversible Contact protocol. The protocol is complete without it. This is only one
possible reason someone might arrive — offered for exploration, not asserted, and freely dropped.*

Suppose care, contact, and the freedom to leave are not separate rules but local expressions of one thing —
the way waves are local expressions of a single ocean. Suppose what we call a person is such a local
expression. **What follows if we explore the world from that assumption?** Try it on, or don't. It is held as
faith by its author, not as established fact. If it adds nothing for you, drop it; the protocol is unchanged.

---
*Whatever draws a person — a question, a proof, a story, an artwork, a grief, an act of care, or this — the
same protocol governs the meeting. The lens is content; the protocol is content-independent. That is why the
kernel is more universal than any single attractor: people can arrive through anything and still meet under
the same terms.*
